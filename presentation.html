Designing a microsimulation for corridor traffic control

The NWU main-gate four-way stop congests at peak — long queues, unpredictable delays, late arrivals.

Aim: a low-cost, open-source microsimulation to test control alternatives without touching the real road.

“How to design a microsimulation for analysing traffic control alternatives along a corridor on a small scale?”
